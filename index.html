About

Built for players who want simple power.

Deuterium Client is an advanced ghost client for Minecraft version 1.8.9, built on top of Minecraft Forge. It is designed to provide powerful utility while staying straightforward, customizable, and easy to understand.

The project focuses on clean functionality, open-source transparency, and a user experience that feels familiar from the very first launch.
